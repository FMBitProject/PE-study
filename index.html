📄 PE Checklist Guideline
Pragmatic Local Pharmacoeconomic Variable Checklist & Filling Instructions
Therapeutic Area: Cardiology  ·  Users: Medical Affair, Clinical Project Leaders, Designated Personnel  ·  Study Types: RCT & Observational Studies
📌 Purpose of This Checklist

This checklist ensures that all essential health economic (HE) and pharmacoeconomic (PE) variables are correctly integrated into the Case Report Form (CRF) of a cardiology clinical trial. It is a structured review tool to be completed by designated personnel before the CRF is finalized and deployed at site level.

👥 Who Should Fill This Checklist?
Medical Affair (MA)
Primary reviewer. Verifies PE variable inclusion, completes sign-off, and performs SDV (Source Data Verification) after review.
Clinical Project Leader (CPL)
Oversees operational workflow. Reviews Section D sign-off items to confirm protocol, CRF architecture, and SIV briefing alignment.
Data Manager / CRO
Confirms eCRF technical integration. Provides input for Section B (EQ-5D-5L structured entry) and Section D CRF Architecture phase.
📅 When to Fill This Checklist?
1
Protocol Development Phase
Fill when the clinical trial protocol is being drafted. Focus on Sections A, B, C — verify all PE variables are written into the protocol data collection plan.
2
CRF Architecture Phase
Fill after the eCRF is built by Data Management. Verify all variables from the checklist are technically implemented and testable in the CRF system.
3
Site Initiation Visit (SIV)
Fill prior to or during the SIV. Confirm investigators have been briefed on EQ-5D patient administration and PE data collection procedures.
📝 How to Fill — Section by Section
A
Macro-Level Resource Utilization
Check each item only if the corresponding field is confirmed to be present in the draft CRF — do not check if the field is planned but not yet implemented.
• A1: Verify admission and discharge date fields exist as separate CRF fields, not just a calculated LoS field.
• A1 (ICU): Check only if the CRF has a dedicated numeric field for ICU/CVCU days — a free-text note does not qualify.
• A2: Confirm the CRF has a checklist or multi-select field enumerating major CV procedures (PCI, CABG, Pacemaker/ICD, Valvular surgery).
• A3: Both the Yes/No readmission field and the etiology categorization must be present. Check etiology item only when readmission data collection is confirmed.
B
HRQoL Utilities (EQ-5D-5L)
Confirms the EQ-5D-5L instrument is properly embedded in the eCRF with all required time points.
• B1 (Instrument): Tick only when the full EQ-5D-5L questionnaire is incorporated — not just a reference to administer it separately.
• Time points (Baseline, Mid-point, EoS): Each must be independently scheduled in the visit schedule/CRF. Tick each confirmed time point separately.
• 5-digit profile: eCRF must accept structured input for all 5 dimensions (MO, SC, UA, PD, AD) — a single free-text field does not qualify.
• VAS: Confirm a separate numeric input field (0–100 scale) exists, distinct from the 5-dimension profile.
C
SAE Economic Impact
Items here are appended to the standard SAE reporting form — do not confuse with the general hospitalization fields in Section A.
• C1 (Mandatory query): The exact hospitalization necessity question must appear on the SAE form — paraphrased versions are not sufficient.
• Yes/No options: Use the nested checkboxes to confirm both response options are available on the form.
• Supplementary days: Only applicable when the answer is "Yes." Confirm the numeric field for additional hospital days exists as a conditional field.
D
Operational Workflow Sign-Off
Section D uses a phase-lock system — only the item corresponding to the selected Study Phase (set in the form header) can be checked.
• Select the Study Phase in the header metadata first — the appropriate Section D item will unlock automatically.
• Status dropdown: Set the workflow status (Done / In Progress / Not Started) for each phase item even when the checkbox is locked — this provides team-wide visibility.
• Checkbox: Only check when the activity is fully complete, not merely in progress.
💡 Strategic Guidelines — Macro-Costing Focus
✅ Include (Mandatory): Length of Stay (LoS), ICU/CVCU days, emergency readmissions, major CV interventions (PCI, CABG, ICD, Valvular), EQ-5D-5L at all time points, SAE hospitalization data.
❌ Exclude: Routine consumables, minor concomitant medications, standard low-cost lab panels, ward-level nursing time, non-hospitalization outpatient visits (unless specified in protocol).
🔐 SDV Process (Source Data Verification)
Only Medical Affair (MA) may perform SDV. Once verified, a submission is locked and cannot be modified by standard users. All SDV actions are recorded in an audit trail.
Step 1Click 🔐 MA Login in the top navigation bar and enter MA credentials.
Step 2Go to the Dashboard. Submissions show an ✅ SDV button when MA is active.
Step 3Click ✅ SDV, optionally add verification notes, then confirm to lock the submission.
UnlockClick 🔓 Unlock (MA only) and provide a mandatory reason — recorded in the audit trail.
DeleteDeleting a locked submission requires MA login, a written reason, and a second confirmation.
